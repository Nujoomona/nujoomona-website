Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy covers the Nujoomona website (nujoomona.com) and Nujoomona Uploader, an internal tool used only by the Nujoomona team to upload and manage videos on the Nujoomona YouTube channel.

1. Who we are

Nujoomona is an independent children's video series operated from Madrid, Spain. Contact: salam@nujoomona.com.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

Nujoomona Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The tool is not offered to the public. It is used only by Nujoomona team members, signed in with the Nujoomona Google account, to act on the Nujoomona channel.

3. Information the tool accesses

  • OAuth tokens for the Nujoomona Google account, so the tool can upload videos and set their metadata (title, description, captions, thumbnail, audience setting, privacy status).
  • Video status information for our own uploads (for example, processing state and privacy status).

The tool does not access, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube users, and it does not read comments.

4. How information is used, stored and shared

  • Tokens are used only to perform the uploads and status checks described above.
  • Tokens are stored encrypted on systems controlled by Nujoomona and are never shared with or sold to third parties.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website or in the tool.

5. Retention and deletion

Stored tokens are deleted when they are no longer needed, and in any case within 30 days of access being revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any data, email salam@nujoomona.com.

6. Children's privacy

Our videos are made for children and are marked "Made for Kids" on YouTube. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not ask viewers to comment, contact us or share personal details.

7. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
