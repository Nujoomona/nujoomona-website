Nujoomona نجومنا

Islamic animated stories for children aged 3 to 8, in Arabic and English.

Nujoomona ("our stars") is an independent YouTube series that teaches young children everyday Islamic manners, values and du'ā through short, gentle animated episodes. Every script is reviewed by a qualified teacher of Islamic studies before production.
